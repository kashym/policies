Privacy Policy — Cellular Automata

Last updated: October 3, 2026
Summary: Cellular Automata does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. It has no internet permission in its release build and contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

Cellular Automata ("the app") is developed by Kashyman ("we", "us"). This privacy policy explains what data the app does — and does not — collect.

Data the app stores

The app saves the cellular automaton grid states you choose to save (e.g. "Glider pattern", "My custom setup") using local on-device storage only. This data:

  • Never leaves your device
  • Is not accessible to us or any third party
  • Is deleted automatically if you uninstall the app

Data we collect

None. The app does not require account creation, does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, or any other sensitive device permission, and does not connect to the internet in its released version.

Third-party services

The app does not integrate any third-party SDKs, analytics providers, or advertising networks.

Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13, because it does not collect information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes (for example, if a future version adds a feature that uses data), this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to: kashyman.dev@gmail.com
